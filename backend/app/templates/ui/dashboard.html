About This Verification Platform & Live Demo

An automated, privacy-preserving, enterprise KYC and loan document fraud detection platform. It evaluates submitted Aadhaar, PAN, Salary Slips, and Bank Statements across 10 specialized verification modules — computing explainable 0–100 trust scores, detecting cross-document anomalies and syndicate fraud rings, and maintaining a tamper-evident SHA-256 cryptographic hash chain ledger.

M1 Field Validators M2 Blur & Completeness M3 Fernet PII Masking M4 RapidFuzz Cross-Doc M5 Income & EMIs M6 Forgery & ELA M7 NetworkX Fraud Ring M8 Jinja2 Explainer M9 0-100 Trust Routing M10 Cryptographic Ledger
