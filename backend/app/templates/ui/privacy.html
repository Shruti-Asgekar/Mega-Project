Privacy Declaration & Statutory Compliance

Commitment to Data Privacy under the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 & UIDAI Guidelines.

1. Privacy by Design: Aadhaar Redaction

In compliance with UIDAI regulations and the Aadhaar and Other Laws (Amendment) Act, our platform never stores, processes, or logs 12-digit plaintext Aadhaar numbers.

  • During extraction, Aadhaar numbers are immediately transformed into masked format (XXXX-XXXX-1234).
  • Cross-applicant duplicate and fraud ring detection is performed strictly using a one-way keyed HMAC (HMAC-SHA256 with a secure server-side salt). The raw identity is mathematically non-recoverable from this fingerprint.

2. Cryptographic Security & At-Rest Encryption

All submitted personal identifiers (applicant names, phone numbers, addresses, account numbers) and stored document payloads are encrypted at rest using AES-128-CBC / Fernet cryptographic keys.

Every uploaded document is cryptographically anchored into a SHA-256 Merkle-like hash chain. Once recorded, document records cannot be modified or re-sequenced without breaking the cryptographic integrity of the entire ledger.

3. Data Retention & Tombstoning (DPDP Act, 2023)

Under statutory data minimization principles, verification documents and applicant PII are retained only as long as necessary for the verification decision:

  • Retention Window: Verification payloads expire after 30 days by default.
  • Cryptographic Tombstones: Upon expiration or user erasure requests, document payloads are permanently destroyed, and a Tombstone record is stamped onto the ledger. This guarantees that cryptographic chain linkage remains verifiable while honoring the complete erasure of personal data.

4. Statutory Data Principal Rights

As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: View all summary data and verification flags associated with your case at any time.
  • Right to Correction: Request human officer re-review if document extraction or quality checks produced false flags.
  • Right to Data Portability: Export a machine-readable JSON copy of your personal data on your Account Settings page.
  • Right to Erasure: Permanently delete your user profile and document records via the right-to-erasure workflow.
